How It Works

Simple, transparentcatering policy.

👥
Minimum 10 Guests

Every package starts at 10 guests — perfect for intimate gatherings or grand celebrations of any size.

📅
Book 1 Week Ahead

Place orders at least one week in advance. Final guest count is due 3 days before your event.

💳
50% Deposit

A non-refundable 50% deposit secures your date and locks in your menu. Balance due on delivery.

🧾
Service & Tax

18% service charge applies for groups of 8+. PST & GST applied separately. All prices in CAD.

🚗
Delivery Available

Pickup is always free. Delivery starts at $50 — final cost depends on distance and order size.

🌱
Dietary Friendly

Gluten-free & vegan options available on request. Please mention allergies or restrictions when booking.
